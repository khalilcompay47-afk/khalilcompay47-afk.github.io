Terms of Service

Hermes Rclone Sync is a personally operated workflow based on Rclone. It is not a commercial service offered to the general public.

Purpose

The workflow synchronizes selected Obsidian Markdown files between devices that the account owner controls and the owner's Google Drive account, with conflict checks and local backups.

Authorized use

Only the Google account owner should authorize the OAuth client. The owner is responsible for choosing which folders to synchronize and ensuring they have permission to access the selected content. The owner must comply with Google's terms and applicable laws.

Risks and availability

Synchronization can be interrupted by network, device, API, or storage failures. Backups and conflict-prevention checks reduce but do not eliminate the risk of data loss. There is no guarantee of continuous service, compatibility, or error-free file recovery. The owner should maintain independent backups of important content.

Third-party software

Google Drive, Rclone, Android, Windows, and Obsidian are separate products governed by their respective terms. This personal workflow is not a Google or Rclone service and does not imply endorsement by those providers.

Privacy and ending access

For details about Google user data, read the Privacy Policy. The owner can stop using the workflow at any time by turning off sync and revoking the client under Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this OAuth client can be sent to the support address shown on the Google authorization consent screen.
