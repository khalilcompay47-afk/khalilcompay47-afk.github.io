Private, personal-use desktop workflow

Keep your knowledge vault in sync across your own devices.

Hermes Rclone Sync is a personal Rclone-based workflow for synchronizing a selected Obsidian Markdown knowledge vault between Windows, Android, and the account owner's Google Drive.

Not a public sign-in service

What it does

The account owner authorizes Google Drive access via Google's OAuth screen. Rclone uses that permission to read, create, update, and compare files in the owner's chosen Google Drive folders. A guarded two-way sync checks for conflicts, retains backups, and stops when safety checks fail.

Google Drive access is requested because this workflow needs to keep files synchronized between devices—not to collect unrelated Google account information. The currently configured Rclone scope permits broader Drive access than the selected vault; the workflow is intended to operate on designated folders only.

What it does not do

It does not provide public accounts, serve advertisements, sell Google user data, or collect information for profiling. It is not a cloud-based AI assistant. Active local databases, credential files, and private memory databases are excluded from the knowledge-vault sync.

Your data and control

Selected vault files are stored on the owner's devices and in the owner's Google Drive account. Authorization credentials are stored in local Rclone configuration files, not on this website. The owner controls the folders, can pause sync, remove files, and revoke authorization in their Google Account.

Data use and privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for the specific data accessed, why Google Drive permissions are needed, how files are stored, and how to revoke access. Read the Terms of Service for this personal-use workflow.

This informational website does not sign users in, run tracking scripts, or store OAuth tokens. It documents a personal automation workflow operated locally by its owner.
